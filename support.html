What you need

The app works with an Entity system: your Entity computer must be running and on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone. Allow the local network permission when asked.

Try demo

No Entity system yet? Use Try demo in the app to explore it with sample sessions.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/BarOdr/entity-companion/issues. Please do not post private recordings or personal information. Include your iOS version and what you were doing.

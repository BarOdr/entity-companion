Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

The Entity companion app collects no data for its developer. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it has no account or server of ours to send anything to.

Where your data lives

Recordings, videos, names, tags and settings stay on your phone and on your own Entity computer, which the app reaches over your local network. Lyrics may also be stored in your own private iCloud, if you use that feature; that data is held by Apple under your Apple account and is not accessible to us.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Local network: to find your Entity computer on the same Wi-Fi (Bonjour) and to sync sessions with it over plain HTTP on that network.
  • Camera: to record a video of your playing, kept with its session.
  • Microphone: to record the phone's sound with each video, so it can be aligned with the session's audio, and to listen when you describe a routing by voice.
  • Speech recognition: to turn your spoken description into text. The app requires on-device recognition whenever your phone supports it for the chosen language. If on-device recognition is not available, iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers for recognition; that is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the app does not receive or store it elsewhere.
  • Bluetooth: to listen to your Entity system, so it can ask you for a name right after you save.
  • Background activity: best-effort syncing, uploading videos, and audio playback with the screen off.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or receive your data. Data leaves your phone only to your own Entity computer, and to your own iCloud and Apple's speech service as described above.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from the phone. Data on your Entity computer or in your iCloud is yours to manage there.
